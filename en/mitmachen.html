Volunteering

Our association thrives on the commitment of its members. Many tasks can be done digitally; some require attending meetings in person.

We are continually looking for committed people to help us carry our work forward with practical support. Every helping hand is welcome!

Roles we are looking to fill

BoardStrategic leadership, attending meetings, public representation.
TreasurerBookkeeping, payments, annual accounts. Basic accounting knowledge an advantage.
SecretaryKeeping minutes and managing documents.
PR & Social MediaTexts, posts, newsletter; basic knowledge of social-media tools.
FundraisingApproaching companies, preparing sponsor materials.
IT / WebMaintaining the website, backups, small technical adjustments.
Project coordination (Uganda)Communication with CSU, project accounting, reporting.
Sponsorship supportManaging sponsorships, contact with sponsors.

Conditions

  • Travel costs are generally not covered; individual exceptions are possible.
  • Knowledge of English is helpful; the working language is German or English.
  • Physical presence in Germany is not strictly required – collaboration takes place virtually and in person (Hamburg and Kaltenkirchen).
  • Ongoing, freelance or project-based involvement is possible.
How to apply: Send your name, email, preferred role, a short motivation and your availability to info@zbk-ev.de. We will get back to you within 14 days.
