Privacy Policy

The protection of your personal data is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and what rights you have.

1. Controller

Zukunft für Bweyas Kinder e.V., Beethovenstraße 11, 24568 Kaltenkirchen, Germany. Email: info@zbk-ev.de

2. Hosting

Our website is operated by Netlify (Netlify, Inc., 512 2nd Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the site is accessed, Netlify processes technically necessary data (including the IP address) in server log files. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, reliable provision). As Netlify may also process data in the USA, the transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

3. Server log files

Automatically collected data includes browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, host name, time of the request and IP address. This data is not merged with other sources (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Cookies & audience measurement

This website does not set any cookies for analysis or marketing purposes and does not build user profiles. For anonymous audience measurement we use “GoatCounter”, a privacy-friendly statistics service that works without cookies and without persistent identifiers and stores no personal data. Only aggregated information is recorded, such as the page visited, the referring page (referrer), rough origin (country) and browser and device type. To distinguish repeat visits within a single day, GoatCounter derives a pseudonymous, daily-changing and non-reversible hash from the IP address and browser signature; the IP address itself is not stored. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a data-minimising analysis of website usage). As no cookies are set and no personal data is stored, consent via a cookie banner is not required. More information at goatcounter.com.

5. Contact form

If you write to us via the contact form, your details are processed to handle your enquiry. The form is submitted via the “Netlify Forms” service (Netlify, Inc.). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (handling an enquiry). We do not pass this data on without your consent.

6. Donation and payment platforms

We link to external donation platforms (including Betterplace, PayPal, GoFundMe). When you click, you leave our website; the privacy terms of the respective provider apply. We do not process payment data via our website itself.

7. Social media

We link to our profiles on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. You are only forwarded to the respective platform once you click a link.

8. Your rights

You have the right to information (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal email to info@zbk-ev.de is sufficient.

9. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the Independent State Centre for Data Protection Schleswig-Holstein (ULD).

Note: This is a courtesy translation. The German version of the privacy policy is the authoritative one.
