The Association

Board & Tasks

The board carries strategic responsibility and ensures the proper administration of funds. It is elected by the general meeting.

Bodies of the association

The association is run by its bodies: the general meeting, the board and the executive board. The executive board represents the association in and out of court and consists of the 1st chairperson, the 2nd chairperson and the treasurer. All important decisions are made transparently and documented.

Finances & controlling

The treasurer keeps the accounts and prepares the annual financial statement. Regular audits and transparent reports ensure that funds are used exclusively for the association's statutory purposes.

Ongoing tasks

Our continuous work includes recording donations, sending donation receipts, maintaining the website, and close coordination with Children Safe Uganda on needs and projects.

We are continually looking for committed people to carry the board and the association's work forward. More on getting involved
