Main project · €27,000

Renovating the kitchen and main hall

Built over 47 years ago, the building housing the kitchen and main hall is badly in need of repair: cracks in the walls, a damaged floor, outdated electrics. Cooking with firewood produces heavy smoke – a serious health burden for the around 50 children and the staff.

We are planning a comprehensive renovation for safe, hygienic conditions. The new kitchen will initially run on gas and be prepared for a later switch to solar power – and at the same time create potential for vocational training in kitchen and catering. Project start: 18 February 2022.
