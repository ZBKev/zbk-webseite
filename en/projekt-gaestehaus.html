Future project · Sustainability

Modernising the guest house

An attractive guest house creates income – and training places.

A modernised guest house can generate income – and at the same time give young people hands-on training in hospitality.

The guest house was originally built to offer visitors and volunteers affordable accommodation right at CSU – instead of expensive hotels – and thereby create local income. For lack of furnishing, however, it has not been able to compete with the surrounding hotels.

The plan

The plan is to modernise it to a competitive standard – with a garden, canteen and conference area. At the same time, the house is to become a practical training ground for young people learning hospitality, kitchen and service skills.

At a glance

Short-term goal
approx. 15 m UGX (≈ €3,750)
Total cost (est.)
approx. 40 m UGX (≈ €10,000)
Potential income
approx. 1.3 m UGX / month
Added value
Training places in hospitality
UGX = Ugandan shillings. Euro figures rounded (€1 ≈ 4,000 UGX).

In this way an existing building becomes a sustainable source of income – and a future prospect for the young people.
