Our supporters & partners

A number of companies and foundations stand by us on a regular basis – with larger contributions or with sponsorships for children. Their commitment gives us reliability and the ability to plan throughout the year.

Foundation

Heller Familienstiftung

The Heller Familienstiftung has supported us for several years with an annual contribution. A reliable partnership for which we are very grateful.
Company

HW HanseInvest GmbH

HW HanseInvest GmbH has been a reliable and committed partner for several years. They have taken on two child sponsorships and are supporting the two orphaned children towards a positive future.
Company

System Verification

System Verification Deutschland has supported us for many years. We are very grateful to the company for making many projects on the ground in Bweya possible.
