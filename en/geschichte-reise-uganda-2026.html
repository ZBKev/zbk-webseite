Travel report · March 2026

Visiting Bweya

Five days on site – with gifts in our luggage and many impressions.

From 3 to 9 March 2026, our chairperson spent five days on site in Bweya together with his wife – with five large bags and suitcases full of gifts for the children in their luggage.

Alongside gifts, shared games and meals, the main focus was the personal exchange with the team of Children Safe Uganda (CSU). Visits to foster families were part of it too – a key aspect of CSU's work in helping children return to a family environment wherever possible.

Supplies for the Children's Home

Together with the team, supplies were bought locally – food and provisions for the Bweya Children's Home. Rice, beans and flour in large quantities: staple foods that secure the children's everyday life.

What we took away

The strongest impression: happy children and a team on the ground that cares with great dedication. At the same time, it became clear where investment is needed – in maintaining the buildings and in projects that make the Children's Home more economically independent in the long term.

We bring back many impressions, new ideas and concrete next steps – and the anticipation that several people have already expressed interest in visiting Bweya themselves in 2026.
