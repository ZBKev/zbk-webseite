Who we are

Supporting the children of Bweya since 2011

Zukunft für Bweyas Kinder e.V. has supported the Bweya Children's Home (Children Safe Uganda) since 2011. We provide education, nutrition and a safe home for orphaned and vulnerable children in Bweya.

Through targeted projects and sustainable measures, we create lasting prospects on the ground – together with our local partners.

Your donation reaches the children.

Because we work exclusively on a voluntary basis, 100 % of your donation goes directly to the children and projects in Uganda.

Why Bweya?

A home for children without support

The Bweya Children's Home takes in children who have no family support. Alongside immediate care, our local partners work on long-term solutions such as agriculture, water supply and vocational training.

Become a sponsor

For €30 a month you accompany a child at the Bweya Children's Home over the long term – with education, food and a safe home. You receive regular updates and build a real, personal connection.

Already 40 sponsors accompany 57 sponsored children – become part of this community.
