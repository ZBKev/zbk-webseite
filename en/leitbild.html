The Association

Mission Statement

The Bweya Children's Home was founded in 1968 by the Ugandan couple Christopher and Irene Mukasa and built with the help of German donations. For various reasons, support had to be discontinued in the 1980s. Despite difficult circumstances, the orphanage continued to operate up to the present day.

“Zukunft für Bweyas Kinder e.V.” was founded on 10 September 2011 in Bad Segeberg as a non-profit support association for the “Bweya Children's Home International” (today “Children Safe Uganda” / CSU). This mission statement provides orientation and strengthens every member's identification with the association.

Vision

We understand that children without the support of a family are among the most vulnerable members of society. We are committed to giving every child the chance for economic, social, physical, psychological and spiritual development. Our vision is that, with our support, Children Safe Uganda can meet these challenges.

Mission

We secure basic needs (school fees, food, staff salaries), support renovation, safety and infrastructure (fencing, water supply, electrics), and back help-towards-self-help projects in services, crafts and agriculture.

Areas of work

  • Care for the children on the ground
  • Funding through member and supporter contributions
  • Oversight and transparent use of funds
  • Practical involvement on site by arrangement

Our values

  • View of humanity: Compassion shaped by Christian values and respect for human dignity.
  • Teamwork: Appreciation and respectful cooperation.
  • Transparency: Comprehensible decisions and documentation.
  • Reliability: Dependability in word and deed.
  • Partnership: Communication on equal terms with our Ugandan partners.
  • Efficiency: Careful and economical use of resources.
  • Safety: Protection of people and data.
  • Communication: Clear contacts and open channels of information.
