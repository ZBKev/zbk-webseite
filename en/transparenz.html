Transparency & Finances

We handle your donations responsibly and transparently.

Your donation reaches the children.

Because we work exclusively on a voluntary basis, 100 % of your donation goes directly to the children and projects in Uganda.

All administrative and organisational tasks in Germany are carried out on a voluntary basis. As a non-profit organisation we act selflessly; our funds are used exclusively for statutory purposes. Donations are tax-deductible under the relevant tax regulations.

2025 annual accounts

Income 2025

At our general meeting on 18 April 2026 we presented the 2025 annual accounts. Our donation income came to €35,717 – carried above all by the sponsorships.

Sponsorships
€20,884 · 58 %
General donations
€8,367 · 23 %
Fundraising & corporate
€3,646 · 10 %
Membership fees
€2,820 · 8 %

Sponsorships are by far our strongest and most reliable source of income – ahead even of membership fees.

100 % reaches the children – our administrative costs

Because the board works purely on a voluntary basis, only unavoidable costs arise in Germany. In 2025 these came to €490 – around 1.4 % of income:

Liability insurance€286
Bank & transfer fees€148
Software€56
Total administrative costs€490 (1.4 %)

These small costs are covered by membership fees – so every euro donated serves the association's purpose: supporting the Bweya Children's Home through our partner organisation Children Safe Uganda (CSU).

Note: 2025 was a transition year. The direct transfer to CSU was newly set up (licensing and a dedicated CSU account). Part of the funds is therefore held as an earmarked reserve for Bweya and flows to Uganda on an ongoing basis.

What does one child cost?

The all-round care of one child at the Bweya Children's Home costs on average €53.88 per month. Here is how the costs break down:

Care & operations
€37.71 · 70 %
Meals
€9.27 · 17 %
Education
€4.92 · 9 %
Health & hygiene
€1.98 · 4 %

The largest item is the all-round care provided by the team on the ground (staff, water, electricity, maintenance). With a sponsorship of €30 a month you already cover more than half of one child's costs.

Our finances are audited each year by two independent auditors elected by the general meeting. The audit for the 2025 financial year was duly completed and reported to the general meeting.
