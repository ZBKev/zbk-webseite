From Bweya · November 2026

Another sustainable investment in the Bweya Children's Home

Something lasting should grow out of a gift – a suggestion from the ground.

Zukunft für Bweyas Kinder e.V. had provided a budget for a special fun day for the children. Moses Ssebaggala, Director of the Bweya Children's Home and of Children Safe Uganda, came back to us with a thoughtful request.

After several months of drought, the rainy season has begun. Moses suggests not spending the money on a single day, but investing it in the future: planting 50 to 100 banana plants. A lovely day could thus become a lasting improvement in food security.

“We have been having drought throughout several months. Since we now have enough rains, we feel that instead of eating this money for just one day, what if we invest it in planting at least 50 to 100 banana plantations. If all goes well and bananas grow well, we can be sure of future food for the children.”— Moses Ssebaggala, Children Safe Uganda

Why this can make a real difference

Bananas are a staple food in Uganda. A plantation of their own would feed the children for years and make the Children's Home a little more independent – exactly the goal we pursue together with CSU: projects that last.

Together with the team on the ground, we are reviewing the proposal. We think it is a wonderful idea to let something lasting grow out of a gift – and we will report back as soon as the first plants are in the ground.
