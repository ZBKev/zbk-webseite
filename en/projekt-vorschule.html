Expanding the nursery school

A modern nursery educates children – and helps sustain the home.

The nursery school at the Bweya Children's Home educates children from across the area – and is at the same time an important source of income for the home. A modernisation is intended to make it the community's first choice once again.

CSU's nursery school provides education for the home's children and for children from Bweya. But the buildings have aged: a rusty roof, cracked floors, no shelter from the weather. As a result, enrolment stays low even though there is space.

What is planned

The plan is renovation and modernisation – a new roof, repair and redesign of the classrooms, new furniture and a welcoming outdoor area. In the longer term, a day-care service and a school bus are to be added to reach more children.

Great potential right on the doorstep

The Bweya community comprises more than 1,200 households with many children aged 3 to 7. Its position on an easily reachable road makes drop-off and pick-up simple – ideal conditions for a well-attended nursery.
