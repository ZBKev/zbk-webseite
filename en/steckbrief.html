The Association

Fact Sheet

Zukunft für Bweyas Kinder e.V. at a glance.

Zukunft für Bweyas Kinder e.V. was founded in September 2011 in Bad Segeberg as a non-profit association. Together with our partner organisation Children Safe Uganda (CSU), we support the Bweya Children's Home – with food, education, health care and support.

Download the fact sheet (PDF, in German)

Why do we exist?

The former chairman spent part of his childhood in Bweya, Uganda, in the early 1970s. His parents were involved in building a children's home that later ran into financial difficulty and asked for support. That is how our association came about.

At the start of our journey, around 100 children were housed in two buildings – a challenging situation for everyone involved. Our goal was and is to secure the children's basic needs in a lasting way and to give them a self-determined future. We want to support all children in the care of Children Safe Uganda with food, education, health care and support.

The Bweya Children's Home offers children who are orphaned or come from unbearable family circumstances a temporary rehabilitation stay of around one year. It ends with a controlled return to the family of origin or placement with a foster family. A new focus of CSU is therefore the search for families of origin and relatives, as well as visiting the children in their new and often hard-to-reach surroundings.

Where do we want to go?

We have set ourselves the shared goal of financial independence from external donors. We want to enable Children Safe Uganda's strategic projects, which generate income while giving the children vocational training. Self-sufficiency in water and electricity and the cultivation of food are intended to reduce monthly expenses.

The renewal of the kitchen complex is currently underway and can in future form the basis for a catering business. Also high on the list are the construction of a B&B and a training centre. For these we are looking for partners and companies willing to take such projects on.

At the same time, the children should grow up in an environment that gives them a warm home alongside safety and education. Families who take in children are supported with a project designed to improve their economic situation in a lasting way – the budget for this is part of our monthly contributions.

What are our challenges?

Rising inflation and the fall of the euro against the local currency in Uganda currently make it extremely difficult for CSU to cover all costs. High unemployment and poverty sadly also lead to more children being abandoned. We have so far been able to cover additional costs for higher school fees and transport – but that budget is then missing for other important investments. The board cannot yet fully take on fundraising and communication.

How are we financed?

ZBK supports the Bweya Children's Home with an annual budget of around €35,000. These funds come from membership fees, sponsorships, individual donations and, recently, partnerships with companies. We pass on all donation income in full to CSU.
